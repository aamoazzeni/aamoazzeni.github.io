Research

Three strands, one question: how humor scales.

My work sits at the intersection of media management, dramaturgy, and applied artificial intelligence. The unifying problem is practical: humor is the cheapest and most volatile content a media organization can produce, and almost nobody has a management theory for it.

01

Humor-Based Media Management

How satirical and comedic media are planned, staffed, funded and governed. My doctoral dissertation builds a management model for humor-producing organizations, treating comedic output as a repeatable production system rather than an individual talent.

02

Comedy Dramaturgy

Between 2016 and 2017 I reverse-engineered a corpus of Iranian and American films, series, animations and stand-up sets to extract the structural grammar of comedy. The result is a framework of six layers and six meta-patterns for designing comic space in narrative.

03

Applied AI & RAG Systems

Private large-language-model assistants and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines for cultural, educational, medical and public-sector organizations — deployed on self-hosted infrastructure with Dify and n8n, and managed through a custom WordPress control panel.

Doctoral Research

Managing humor-based media

Humor-Based Media Management

A study of how comedic media products are organized, produced and sustained. The dissertation connects the dramaturgical structure of humor to the managerial decisions that make it possible at scale — commissioning, team composition, editorial risk, distribution and audience feedback.
